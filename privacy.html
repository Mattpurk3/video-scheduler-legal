Privacy Policy Effective Date: July 27, 2026 This Privacy Policy describes how the App, a personal automation tool operated by Matthew Purkey, handles data. 1. Data Collected The App stores: (a) an OAuth access token and open ID issued by TikTok upon the owner's explicit authorization, used solely to publish video content on the owner's behalf; (b) video files and captions the owner adds to the App's local publishing queue. 2. How Data Is Used Data is used exclusively to publish the owner's own content to the owner's own TikTok account on a schedule set by the owner. The App does not use this data for any other purpose. 3. Data Storage All data is stored locally on the owner's own device. The App does not transmit data to any third party other than TikTok's official API, as required to publish content. 4. Data Sharing The App does not sell, share, or disclose any data to third parties. 5. Data Retention & Deletion The owner may delete stored tokens or queued content at any time by removing them from local storage. Revoking the App's access via TikTok's account settings immediately invalidates the App's ability to post. 6. Contact matthewpurkey5@gmail.com
